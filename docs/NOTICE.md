# Notices

- `orbital.clock` and `orbital.workspaces` are modified clones of the built-in `omarchy.clock` / `omarchy.workspaces`
  (Omarchy, MIT).
- `orbital.dock` started from the `arc.dock` plugin (claudsondouglas); check its license before publishing.
- Not bundled: the floating bar plugin (`charlieras262.floating-bar`, whose gap scale was patched locally in the author's setup),
  Omaland, and the lock-screen fork. The theme works with the stock bar.
